# Account Management

## Creating an Account

You can create an account by clicking "Sign Up" with your name, email, and password, or during checkout by checking "Save my information."

Social login is available via Google or Apple. If you use social login with an email that already has an account, it will link to your existing account automatically.

## Password Reset

To reset a forgotten password:

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
2. Enter your account email address.
3. A reset link will arrive within a few minutes (check spam if not).
4. The link is valid for 24 hours.
5. Your new password must be at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

We cannot retrieve your current password — only reset it.

## Changing Your Email Address

To change your email, go to Account Settings, click "Edit" next to your email, and enter your current password to confirm. A verification link is sent to the new address and must be clicked to complete the change. Until verified, your account keeps using the old email. Verification links expire after 48 hours.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA adds a one-time code requirement on every login. To enable it, go to Account Settings > Security and choose SMS verification or an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy). We strongly recommend enabling 2FA if you have saved payment methods.

## Saved Addresses

You can save multiple shipping addresses under Account Settings > Addresses. One address can be set as your default and will be pre-filled at checkout.

## Saved Payment Methods

Payment methods are saved as encrypted tokens through our payment processor — your full card number is never stored on our servers. Manage saved cards under Account Settings > Payment Methods.

## Order History

Your full order history is available under Orders. For each order you can view the itemized receipt, track shipment status, start a return, download a PDF invoice, or re-order the same items. Order history is retained indefinitely for active accounts.

## Notification Preferences

Manage email and SMS notifications under Account Settings > Notifications.

Available types:
- **Order confirmations** — cannot be disabled
- **Shipping updates** — tracking numbers and delivery confirmations
- **Promotional emails** — sales, new arrivals, discount codes
- **Back-in-stock alerts** — for saved items
- **CSAT surveys** — after order delivery

To unsubscribe from marketing emails, click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email, or disable them in account settings. Unsubscribing takes effect within 48 hours. Transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates) cannot be disabled.

## Social Login Management

To link or unlink a Google or Apple login, go to Account Settings > Security > Connected Accounts. You can only unlink a social login if you have a password set on your account.

## Account Deletion

To delete your account, contact support. A 30-day grace period applies — your account is deactivated but not deleted, and you can cancel the request during this window. Pending orders will still be fulfilled, and refunds/returns for prior orders will still be honored. After 30 days, deletion is permanent and irreversible. Order history cannot be recovered.

## Accessibility

Our website supports standard browser accessibility features. Contact support if you need accommodations or encounter accessibility issues.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Account Management

### How is my personal data used?

Your data (name, email, address, purchase history) is used to process orders, provide support, and send communications you've opted into. We do not sell personal data to third parties. We are GDPR compliant for customers in the European Economic Area. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
