# Payment Methods and Billing

## Accepted Payment Methods

Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover. Cards must have a valid billing address on file.

Digital wallets: PayPal, Apple Pay (Safari/iOS), Google Pay (Chrome/Android).

Store credit and gift cards can be applied at checkout. Gift cards are available in $25, $50, $100, and $200 denominations.

## When You Are Charged

Your payment method is charged when your order ships, not when you place it. If items ship separately, you are charged per shipment. A pre-authorization hold may appear on your statement at order time but is released if the order is cancelled or adjusted before shipment.

## Receipts and Invoices

An itemized email receipt is sent within 1 hour of shipment, including products, taxes, shipping, and discounts. You can also download a PDF invoice from Order History at any time. For a formal invoice with business details, contact support with your order number.

## Failed Payments

Common causes: incorrect billing address, insufficient funds, card flagged by your bank, or expired card.

Steps to resolve:
1. Verify card number, expiration, and CVV.
2. Confirm billing address matches your bank's records exactly.
3. Contact your bank to check for transaction blocks.
4. Try a different payment method.

If payment fails after order placement (during capture at shipment), we'll email you to update payment details. The order is held for 48 hours before automatic cancellation.

## Promo Codes

Enter promo codes in the "Discount Code" field at checkout and click "Apply." Rules:
- One code per order — codes cannot be stacked.
- Codes cannot be combined with other offers unless explicitly stated.
- Codes are case-insensitive.
- Expired or invalid codes show an error at checkout.
- Codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.

## Price Matching

We price match within 14 days of purchase if you find the same item cheaper at a competitor's authorized retailer. The item must be identical (model, size, color) and in stock at the competitor at the time of your request. Marketplace sellers and auction sites do not qualify. Contact support with a link to the competitor listing and your order number. Approved matches are refunded as store credit or to your original payment method.

## Price Adjustments

If an item you purchased goes on sale within 14 days, you can request a one-time price adjustment. The difference is refunded to your original payment method. This does not apply to items purchased during limited-time promotions or flash sales.

## Sales Tax

Tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address and state tax laws. The final amount is shown before you confirm. We collect and remit tax in all required states. Tax-exempt organizations should contact support before ordering to set up a tax-exempt account.

## Currency

All prices are in US Dollars (USD). International buyers' banks handle currency conversion at their own exchange rate. We are not responsible for foreign transaction fees.

## Payment Security

All payment data is encrypted via TLS and processed through PCI-DSS compliant processors. Saved payment methods are stored as secure tokens — we never store full card numbers.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Payments

### Why was I charged a different amount than shown at checkout?

The most common reasons: sales tax calculated on your shipping address, currency conversion fees from your bank (for non-USD accounts), or a temporary pre-authorization hold that differs from the final charge. If none of these explain it, contact support with your order number and bank statement detail.

### Do you have a loyalty or rewards program?

Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches.

### Can I purchase in bulk for my business?

Yes. Contact support with the items and quantities you need. Bulk orders require a minimum of 10 units per SKU. Orders over $1,000 get a dedicated account manager.

### Are your products authentic?

Yes. All products are sourced directly from authorized manufacturers or their official distributors. We do not sell third-party marketplace items, grey-market goods, or refurbished products unless explicitly labeled. Contact support with your order number if you have concerns about a specific product.
